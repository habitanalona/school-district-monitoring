      Restricted measurements recorded by authorized school health personnel. Visible only to accounts with health access.

      Health workspace
      Restricted record. Height, weight, BMI, and nutritional status are sensitive personal information under the Data Privacy Act. Do not print, export, or share this section without authorization.

      BMI is computed from valid measurements. Nutritional status is an authorized assessment; the system does not apply adult BMI cutoffs to children.
